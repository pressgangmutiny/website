Biography

Pressgang Mutiny are Toronto’s “delightfully unhinged and uniquely talented” shantymen. Richard Kott, Josh Ludy, James McKie and Stefan Read sing in four-part harmony, with beats and live instruments drawing on hip hop, drum & bass, reggae, dancehall and soca.

The band formed in 2013 and has spent more than a decade learning where shanties came from, who sang them and what they still have to say. In Barrouallie, St Vincent and the Grenadines, they learned directly from George “Tall 12” Frederick and filmed a documentary about him and the young people carrying his songs forward. They are tradition-bearers who take that responsibility seriously, with plenty of ideas of their own to contribute.

Black sailors and Caribbean and African-American work-song traditions helped shape shanties. Pressgang’s collaborations with reggae and hip hop artists explore that shared heritage. Their third album, Departure (2026), features Carl Harvey of Toots and the Maytals and reggae MC Sunray Grennan. “Hill and Gully / Sally Rackett” reached 132 stations, programmers and syndicated shows in more than 30 countries.

Live, Josh DJs and sings, the four members trade leads and harmonies, and concertina, fiddle, guitar, spoons and bones sit alongside the beats. Audiences learn the responses and sing with the band. For workshops, Pressgang pull the harmonies apart and teach the songs and their histories. They developed the shanty curriculum for Goderich Celtic Roots College and lead community sings for all ages.

In August 2026, they played 31 shows in 29 days across the UK, including Sidmouth Folk Festival, FolkEast, Whitby Folk Week and Shrewsbury Folk Festival, and sold every CD they brought. Other appearances include Paimpol in France, Port Pieśni Pracy in Poland, the Chi Shanty and Fundy festivals in New Brunswick, and showcases at Folk Music Ontario and Folk Alliance International.
