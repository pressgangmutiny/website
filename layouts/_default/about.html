The Band

About

We are Pressgang Mutiny, Toronto’s “delightfully unhinged and uniquely talented” shantymen. We’re musicians and sailors who have spent more than a decade learning where these songs came from, who sang them and why, and what they still have to say.

Along the way, we’ve found shared musical roots with hip hop, soca, dancehall and reggae. Those connections run through our records and our live shows. Josh DJs and sings, the four of us sing in four-part harmony and trade the lead, and we play concertina, fiddle, guitar, spoons and bones. We’ll teach you the choruses. You can take it from there.

We’re tradition-bearers, and we’ve worked to earn that responsibility by learning directly from those who came before us. In Barrouallie, St Vincent and the Grenadines, we learned from George “Tall 12” Frederick and filmed a documentary about him and the young people carrying his songs forward. We take our place in that chain seriously, and we want to leave the next singers plenty of room for their own ideas.

Our third album, Departure (2026), includes Carl Harvey of Toots and the Maytals and reggae MC Sunray Grennan. “Hill and Gully / Sally Rackett” reached 132 stations, programmers and syndicated shows in more than 30 countries. In August 2026 we played 31 shows in 29 days across the UK, including Sidmouth, FolkEast, Whitby and Shrewsbury, and sold every CD we brought.

We teach harmony and shanty workshops and lead community sings. We also developed the shanty curriculum for Goderich Celtic Roots College.

We are Richard Kott, Josh Ludy, James McKie and Stefan Read, and we love singing together.

The Four of Us

Members

Richard Kott

Vocals, Guitar, Spoons

Richard is a lifelong singer, chorister and a cappella bass who also plays guitar and piano. In Pressgang, he’s often hanging around a low C having basso profundo fun, or leading a ballad about love and loss. He helps develop the band’s workshops.

Josh Ludy

Vocals, DJ

Josh sings and DJs with Pressgang Mutiny and performs as DJ Trulii. He studied at Berklee College of Music in Boston.

James McKie

Vocals, Fiddle, Production, Rhythm Bones

Juno Award-winning multi-instrumentalist, composer and producer James McKie produced Pressgang’s first three albums. He has recorded with Cowboy Junkies, toured across five continents and completed cultural residencies in Chile and Colombia. He holds an MA in Music and a BFA.

Stefan Read

Vocals, Concertina, Melodeon

Stefan is a second-generation folk musician and dancer. He sings and plays concertina and melodeon, performs with Sweet Felons All, and organises Another Bloody Folk Club in Toronto. He produces The Shanty Show.

Why Shanties

Where the Songs Came From

Shanties helped sailors work together aboard merchant and whaling ships. The crews came from many countries and brought their music with them. Black sailors and Caribbean and African-American work-song traditions helped shape this music, though you wouldn’t always know it from the way shanties are presented today.

We want more people to hear that history. It’s part of what we teach in workshops, and it’s why we make records with reggae and hip hop artists. There’s a shared musical heritage to explore together.

On The Shanty Show, we ask singers and researchers to share what they know. Those conversations keep teaching us things we bring back to the band.
