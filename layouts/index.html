The Band

Tradition-bearers

We are Pressgang Mutiny, Toronto’s “delightfully unhinged and uniquely talented” shantymen. We are musicians dedicated to challenging conventional understanding of sea shanties from a place of deep immersion in their roots.

We’ve travelled to discover where this work-song tradition came from, who sang it and why, and what it still has to say. Its often-erased Black and Caribbean roots inform the music we make today, from four-part harmony to our collaborations with reggae and hip hop artists.

We are tradition-bearers. We want our generation to contribute to the tradition and pass it on to people who will have their own ideas about what to do with it. That gives us plenty of room to experiment.

Our Story

Latest Release

Departure

On Departure, we explore the shared musical heritage of shanties, hip hop, soca, ska and dancehall with musicians who know those genres from the inside. Our voices meet samples, drum machines and new arrangements of songs we’ve spent years singing.

Listen to “Hill and Gully / Sally Rackett”: call and answer vocal lines with veteran Toronto reggae drummer and deejay Sunray Grennan make those connections audible. The album also features Carl Harvey of Toots and the Maytals, Al Cross and Terry Wilkins (Big Sugar’s original rhythm section), and melodic rapper, lyricist and vocalist Matt Somber.

We’re back in the studio, recording our next album.
